Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the random picker guarantee fairness?

Kuji uses cryptographically sound pseudo-random algorithms (PRNG) powered by the browser's crypto and Math.random engines to ensure unbiased entropy.

Can I use custom weights?

Yes! Enable Custom Weights in the sidebar to assign weights from 1x to 10x, giving specific options higher or lower mathematical odds.

Can I share my custom board?

Yes! Click 'Share Board' to generate a shareable URL containing your exact choices and generator mode to share with friends, students, or colleagues.
